Less friction. Not less thought.

Good tools don't do
the reading for you.

They make it easier to stay with a difficult idea. Mengshee is built for professional scientists and engineers who read closely: checking a derivation, returning to a classic paper, comparing passages, or preparing corrections for a colleague.

Its job is to reduce the effort of handling documents—not the intellectual work of understanding them.

Made for the research desk

Five ways to stay
with the work.

From the first careful reading
to the copy you share.

01 / ANNOTATE

Put the mathematics
in the margin.

A highlight marks a passage. A LaTeX Note lets you work through it. Add typeset formulas, derivations, and technical explanations directly beside the text.

Rendered with StemTeX and saved as standard PDF Stamp annotations, notes preserve both editable source and their visible appearance. Other readers can display them without installing Mengshee PDF.

  • LaTeX notes and callouts
  • Editable source, saved inside the PDF
  • Custom fonts, packages, and preambles
02 / FOLLOW THE SOURCES

Old literature.
A better way in.

Classic papers deserve more than a static scan. Edit internal links, named destinations, and the table of contents. Use OCR to recognize text or correct an existing OCR text layer.

Open references in fully functional auxiliary reading panes—not just previews. Navigate, select text, and annotate without losing your place in the main document.

  • Editable cross-references and OCR text
  • Splittable auxiliary reading panes
  • Independent reading positions, ordinary annotation tools
03 / PROOFREAD

Make corrections
easy to act on.

Dedicated proofreading tools connect each correction to its place in the paper. Add numbered LaTeX callouts, edit their internal IDs, and choose document-wide, per-page, or custom numbering.

Renumber explicitly when needed, then export an Excel-compatible CSV with page, ID, displayed number, and LaTeX source. Share the annotated paper and a structured correction checklist.

  • Independent IDs and visible numbering
  • Numbering formats saved in the PDF
  • Whole-document CSV export
04 / READ BY VIEWS

A smaller screen.
Not a smaller idea.

Define numbered regions on a page and read them in sequence. Follow a paper's columns or focus on part of a large page, without constantly navigating the whole sheet.

Views are display ranges, not new PDF pages. They do not split or crop the file. Text selection, navigation, and annotation remain available; switch back to the whole page whenever you want.

  • User-defined regions, saved with the PDF
  • Apply a page's layout across the document
  • Keep annotating while reading by Views
05 / SHARE

Your PDF should travel.
Without your software.

Flattening is not required. A PDF saved in Mengshee is designed to display in standards-compliant readers, including its LaTeX notes and numbered badges. Your collaborator should not need Mengshee PDF to see your work.

For extra reassurance—or a reader with limited annotation support—export images or a separate flattened PDF. Keep the editable original.
