## Create corrections

Switch to **Proofread** mode and place a **Numbered Callout** beside the passage you want to correct. Enter the explanation; mathematical content can use LaTeX. Position the box and its leader so that the target remains clear.

![Numbered proofreading notes](/mengshee/assets/proofreading.png)

## Number and export

Choose the numbering format before using **Click to Number**. Available arrangements include a document-wide sequence, a sequence on each page, and page-plus-counter labels. Click the callouts in the order you want readers to follow.

The internal identity of a correction and its displayed number are different. Moving a note does not mean you have deliberately changed its place in the review sequence. Review the displayed numbers after reorganizing a page.

Export the correction list as CSV when you need to share it with an author or manage it in a spreadsheet. It includes page references, internal identifiers, displayed numbers, and LaTeX source. Save the PDF as well: the CSV is a list of corrections, not a replacement for their visual placement.

For an ordinary explanation without a numbered review workflow, use a [LaTeX note or callout](../latex-notes/).
