Mengshee is a PDF reading and annotation workspace for scientific and engineering work. Use it to follow references, write mathematics beside a paper, organize corrections, and prepare documents for sharing.

This guide covers **2026.0.26.9** on Windows. English and Simplified Chinese have the same chapters. Use the language selector to switch to the corresponding page. Application labels may vary with your interface language; this guide names the relevant tool rather than relying on its icon alone.

## A useful distinction

Reading settings change how you see a document. Page edits and annotations change what you save. **Read by Views** does not crop the PDF; changing a page's size does. Automatic paper orientation turns the output sheet, not the saved PDF page.

Save your work as a PDF to keep edits. Keep an editable original when exporting a flattened PDF or images. Recipients can view rendered LaTeX notes without installing StemTeX, but editing their source requires Mengshee and its rendering runtime.
